✍️ Templates de Writing

Modelos prontos para copiar, adaptar e usar no EF SET. Clique em "Copiar"!

📧 Email de apresentação
Dear [Name], My name is [Your Name] and I am [age] years old. I live in [City, Country]. I am a [student/worker] and I study/work at [place]. In my free time, I like to [hobby 1] and [hobby 2]. I also enjoy [hobby 3] with my friends. I am writing because [reason]. I would like to [objective]. Please let me know if you need more information. Best regards, [Your Name]
Dica: Substitua tudo entre [ ] pelos seus dados reais ou fictícios.
📧 Email de reclamação
Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to complain about [product/service] that I bought/used on [date]. Unfortunately, [describe the problem]. This is unacceptable because [reason]. I would like a [refund/replacement/solution]. I hope you can resolve this issue as soon as possible. I look forward to hearing from you. Yours faithfully, [Your Name]
Dica: Sempre seja educado, mesmo reclamando. "I would like" é melhor que "I want".
📧 Email pedindo informações
Dear [Name], I am writing to ask for some information about [topic/course/product]. I would like to know: - How much does it cost? - When does it start? - Where is it located? - What do I need to bring? Could you please send me more details? I am very interested in [topic]. Thank you for your time. Best regards, [Your Name]
Dica: Use bullet points para organizar suas perguntas. Fica mais claro!
📧 Email de confirmação
Dear [Name], I am writing to confirm my [reservation/appointment/booking] for [date]. I would like to confirm the following details: - Name: [Your Name] - Date: [Date] - Time: [Time] - Number of people: [Number] Please let me know if there is any problem. Thank you very much. Best regards, [Your Name]
Dica: Sempre confirme dados importantes (nome, data, hora) por escrito.
📝 Descrição de rotina diária
I usually wake up at [time] every day. First, I [activity 1]. Then, I [activity 2]. After that, I [activity 3]. I usually have [meal] at [time]. In the afternoon, I [activity 4]. In the evening, I like to [activity 5]. I usually go to bed at [time]. On weekends, my routine is different. I [weekend activity].
Dica: Use "usually", "sometimes", "always" para variar. Present simple é a chave!
📝 Descrição de imagem
In the picture, I can see [main subject]. There is/are [objects/people] in the [place]. The [person/thing] is [verb+ing]. It looks like [description]. In the background, there is/are [background elements]. The weather seems [adjective]. I think this picture was taken in [place] because [reason].
Dica: Comece com "In the picture, I can see..." e descreva do primeiro plano ao fundo.
📝 Descrição de problema passado
Last [day/week/month], I had a problem with [situation]. I was [verb+ing] when suddenly [what happened]. At first, I felt [emotion]. Then, I decided to [solution]. It was not easy, but finally I [result]. I learned that [lesson]. Next time, I will [future plan].
Dica: Use past continuous (was/were + verb+ing) para a ação em andamento e past simple para a interrupção.
📝 Opinião / Argumentação
In my opinion, [topic] is [adjective]. I believe this for several reasons. First of all, [reason 1]. For example, [example]. Secondly, [reason 2]. This is important because [explanation]. However, some people think that [counter-argument]. I understand this point, but [your response]. In conclusion, I think that [summary of opinion]. I hope that [future hope].
Dica: "In my opinion", "First of all", "Secondly", "However", "In conclusion" são conectores essenciais.
📝 Comparação de opções
I am comparing [Option A] and [Option B]. Both have advantages and disadvantages. [Option A] is [adjective 1] and [adjective 2]. It is also [comparison]. However, it is [disadvantage]. On the other hand, [Option B] is [adjective 3]. It is [comparison 2] than [Option A]. But it can be [disadvantage 2]. After thinking about both options, I prefer [Option] because [reason]. I think it is the best choice for me.
Dica: Use comparativos: better than, more expensive than, cheaper than, easier than.
